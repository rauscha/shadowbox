Every method here casts a shadow of the data and asks you to judge the object from it. These pages let you move the light.

Each rung is an interactive essay: the figures are instruments you drag, and every idea is taught twice — first on synthetic data where the truth is known and drawn on screen, then on real clinical data where it isn't. The contrast is the lesson.
