Statistical methods look at your data from one particular angle and report what they see from there. Change the angle and the answer changes. These pages are about the angle.

Each one is a chapter you can put your hands on. The figures aren’t pictures of results, they’re working instruments: drag the line, drag the points, watch the numbers follow. Every idea gets taught twice, first on synthetic data where the true answer is known and drawn on screen, then on clinical data where nobody knows it.
